Q84 records all 40 ranks for each registered ex-ante strategy and target using strict-prior histories. It reconciles Top5/Top10/Top20 with the existing historical rule ledger and records lifecycle, recurrence, pre-target state, equivalence, redundancy, complementarity, antagonism, marginal contribution, null, and retrospective-only classifications. Its 42 registered strategies are a finite rule grammar, not an exhaustive universe of mathematical formulas.

Automated acceptance leaves Q84 pending human review. No strategy is promoted automatically and no historical freeze is modified.
