## New quantitative hypothesis

Prior-only rolling behaviour of the six Q80 per-number formula families might help forecast the next target's mean rank percentile across the fixed 1,023-formula Q7 quintet universe, beyond Q7's own rolling-50 rank-quality baseline.

## Decision and limitations

No cross-granularity transfer lift demonstrated. Do not tune this model on the historical Blind OOS, do not promote a selector, and do not insert shadow forecasts.

The outcome is Q7 formula-universe rank quality, not the winning quintet. Historical Blind OOS has prior SONAR exposure and is not virgin prospective evidence. Q80 input is only six coarse family aggregates, not the complete 59-feature grammar.

Only after that should a new pre-target anticipation hypothesis be registered.
