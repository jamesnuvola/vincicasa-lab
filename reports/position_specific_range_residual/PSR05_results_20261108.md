## Hypothesis and formula

For each sorted position i, condition the next marginal on its preceding signed displacement d_i(t)=P_i(t−1)−P_i(t−2). The seven fixed states are d<=-3, d=-2, d=-1, d=0, d=1, d=2, d>=3.
Exact order-statistic prior pi_i(n)=C(n−1,i−1)C(40−n,5−i)/C(40,5), n=i..35+i.
Global comparator qG=(c_i,t(n)+alpha*pi_i(n))/(N_t+alpha); conditional candidate qS=(c_i,t,s(n)+alpha*pi_i(n))/(N_t,s+alpha). Alpha is selected per position only on validation.
