## Hypothesis and formula

Treat each sorted position as its own series. The state for target P_i(t) is the preceding draw's same position P_i(t-1), discretized by four equal-probability bins from the fixed analytical order-statistic prior:

pi_i(n)=C(n-1,i-1) C(40-n,5-i) / C(40,5), n=i,...,35+i.

q_global=(c_i,t(n)+alpha*pi_i(n))/(N_t+alpha); q_state=(c_i,t,s(n)+alpha*pi_i(n))/(N_t,s+alpha).

The candidate is the state-conditioned marginal; comparator is global empirical shrinkage with the same alpha and same prior-only training targets. Positive Brier/log-loss improvement means the conditional candidate scores better. The exact prior is also retained as a reference.

## Root-cause diagnosis

The analytical order-statistic prior is already highly informative about each position's marginal support. Lag-1 conditioning creates state-specific 36-outcome counts and adds variance. Even with the validation-selected shrinkage (alpha=500 for all five positions), the blind OOS Brier gains are not stable. This agrees with PSR-01's min/max range result (high coverage largely expected from the exact prior) and PSR-02's previous-draw odd-count-conditioned positional marginals (all five blind Brier improvements negative, Holm-adjusted p=1).
