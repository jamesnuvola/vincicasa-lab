## Quantitative hypothesis and model

The previous draw's sum is discretized using exact combinatorial quartiles of all 658,008 uniform 5-of-40 combinations. The state cutpoints are 86, 102, 119; state masses are 26.215%, 23.785%, 25.077%, 24.923%.

For position i and target t:

q(i,t,n|s) = [C(i,s,t-1,n) + alpha*pi_i(n)] / [N(i,s,t-1)+alpha]

where pi_i(n)=choose(n-1,i-1)*choose(40-n,5-i)/choose(40,5). The state predictor is the previous draw sum. Validation selects alpha from 300, 1000, 3000 per position, maximizing Brier improvement vs the global transition marginal with the same alpha; ties prefer larger alpha.

## Root-cause diagnosis and limits

The conditional estimator uses only a subset of prior transitions in each previous-sum state. With four sum bins and five sorted-position marginals, sparse state-specific counts are shrunk strongly toward the exact prior. Discovery and validation patterns are not treated as evidence of persistence unless blind OOS is concordant and survives multiplicity correction. The blind tail has been examined in earlier SONAR work; this is not independent confirmation. The marginal model does not define a joint quintet law and provides no evidence that Navier-Stokes dynamics govern the lottery.
