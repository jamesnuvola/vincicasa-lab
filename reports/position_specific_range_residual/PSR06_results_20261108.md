## Hypothesis and formula
Condition each next ordered-position marginal on the range of the preceding draw. Exact range distribution Pr(R=r)=(40-r)C(r-1,3)/C(40,5), r=4..39. Theoretical quartile cutpoints: **23, 28, 33**; state masses: 27.99%, 24.28%, 27.32%, 20.40%.

For position i, exact prior pi_i(n)=C(n-1,i-1)C(40-n,5-i)/C(40,5). Conditional qS=[c_i,t,s(n)+alpha*pi_i(n)]/[N_t,s+alpha]; global comparator qG=[c_i,t(n)+alpha*pi_i(n)]/[N_t+alpha]. Alpha grid 50, 100, 200, 500, selected per position on validation by Brier improvement vs global with same alpha.
