## Hypothesis and formula

For sorted VinciCasa draw (X_{(1)}<\dots<X_{(5)}), the exact uniform-combination marginal for position (i) is
\[
\pi_i(n)=\frac{\binom{n-1}{i-1}\binom{40-n}{5-i}}{\binom{40}{5}},\quad n=i,\dots,35+i.
\]
PSR-01 combines prior-only position counts with fixed preregistered shrinkage (alpha=20):
\[
q_{i,t}(n)=\frac{c_{i,t}(n)+20\pi_i(n)}{N_t+20}.
\]
Past min/max (L_i(t),U_i(t)) are calculated only from rows before target. They are diagnostics, not guaranteed bounds. The candidate's proper scores are compared with the exact order-statistic prior; empirical and posterior Top-1 modes are compared with the theoretical prior mode.

## Range diagnostic / root cause

Blind OOS observed range coverage is 100% at all five positions, but the exact prior already expects:
- P1/P5: 99.991489% (escape mass 0.008511%);
- P2/P4: 99.994529% (escape mass 0.005471%);
- P3: 100%.

Mean past range widths are P1=32, P2=35, P3=36, P4=35, P5=32 versus valid support widths 36,36,36,36,36. The apparent high coverage is therefore largely explained by extremely rare order-statistic tails; no predictive range lift is demonstrated. P3 has no range reduction at all. This is the principal root cause: historical extrema add little exclusion beyond the exact positional geometry, while marginal frequency estimates do not improve the prior's proper scores.
