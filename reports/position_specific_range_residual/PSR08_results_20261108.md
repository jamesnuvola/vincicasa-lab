## Hypothesis and exact pair prior

For adjacent ordered positions i and i+1, the exact uniform 5-subset pair prior is

\\(\\pi_i(a,b)=\\frac{\\binom{a-1}{i-1}\\binom{40-b}{4-i}}{\\binom{40}{5}},\\quad 1\\le a<b\\le40.\\)

The conditioning feature is the previous draw's adjacent gap G_i=P_(i+1)-P_i. Exact integer-count quartiles of the gap prior yield cutpoints **[3, 5, 10]** for all four adjacent pairs.

| Gap state | Definition | Exact prior mass |
|---|---|---:|
| 1 | gap ≤ 3 | 33.755060729% |
| 2 | 4–5 | 16.909368883% |
| 3 | 6–10 | 27.678386889% |
| 4 | gap ≥ 11 | 21.657183499% |

The conditional model is \\(q_{i,t}(x\\mid s)=\\frac{c_{i,t,s}(x)+\\alpha\\pi_i(x)}{N_{t,s}+\\alpha}\\); its comparator is global pair-joint shrinkage using the same alpha and training prefix. Alpha is selected only on validation from 100, 300, 1000 by mean multiclass Brier improvement, ties to the smaller alpha.

The discovery-to-blind reversal is compatible with unstable association/sampling noise; it is not evidence of a predictive edge.

## Diagnosis, limitations, next action

- Discovery Brier gains are positive for all four adjacent pairs, while validation is mixed and every blind-OOS Brier gain is negative.
- The temporal reversal is consistent with unstable historical association and sampling noise; no pair survives Holm correction.
- Each pair has 666 supported joint cells; conditioning on four previous-gap states reduces the available training observations per cell, while alpha=1000 dominates the selected shrinkage grid.
- Some blind-OOS log-loss gains are slightly positive, but the secondary metric is not concordant with the primary Brier endpoint and does not alter the decision.

- This protocol was not formally persisted before the initial computation; treat PSR-08 as exploratory.
- The historical blind slice overlaps earlier SONAR lab research and is not independent lab-wide confirmation.
- Four pairwise joint distributions do not define or validate the full 5-number joint ticket.
- The conditional state is a prior-draw association diagnostic, not a causal effect.
- The discrete statistical analogy is not a physical Navier-Stokes field and does not claim fluid dynamics governs the draw.
