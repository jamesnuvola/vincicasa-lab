## Hypothesis and formula

For sorted position i, use a rolling empirical marginal smoothed by the exact uniform 5-of-40 order-statistic prior:

\(q_{i,t,W,\alpha}(n)=[C_{i,t,W_{eff}}(n)+\alpha\pi_i(n)]/(W_{eff}+\alpha)\), with \(W_{eff}=\min(W,t)\).

\(\pi_i(n)=\binom{n-1}{i-1}\binom{40-n}{5-i}/\binom{40}{5}\).

The validation-selected candidate is compared with both the exact prior and the cumulative empirical position marginal with the same alpha. All predictions use strictly prior rows.

## Diagnosis, limitations, decision

- This experiment isolates a recency effect in one-dimensional position marginals; PSR-07/08 conditioned on previous-draw states and pairwise joint cells, so the hypothesis is distinct.
- Selection is validation-only, but the archive blind segment was inspected in prior SONAR work; results are retrospective and not independent confirmation.
- Position order-statistic priors are nonuniform by construction; comparing only raw hit rates would confound intrinsic position difficulty, so the primary endpoint is multiclass Brier against the exact prior.
- The cumulative empirical same-alpha comparator controls whether any apparent gain is attributable specifically to recency rather than simply empirical deviations from the theoretical prior.
- Cross-runtime replay initially exposed sub-ULP Math.log drift in descriptive log-loss fields; report JSON values are canonicalized to 12 decimals and log-loss ledger gains to 9 decimals.
- Historical data cannot demonstrate a physical fluid-dynamics mechanism or lottery predictability.
- Five marginal laws do not describe the dependence among all five ordered numbers.
- Window/alpha selection may overfit validation; blind test is historical and previously seen by other project analyses.
- No prospective Q60 gate completion; no engine promotion or ticket recommendation.

**Decision:** No promotion. Record the position-specific marginal results as exploratory; retain the exact order-statistic prior unless a position endpoint survives Holm and the prospective Q60 gate later passes.
